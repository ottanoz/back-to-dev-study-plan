# ROADMAP – Developer Guide (90-Day Study Plan, 3h/day)

A complete, detailed, and structured guide for your 90-day journey back into professional software development and preparation for international job opportunities.

This document includes:

- 90-day study plan (3 hours per day)  
- Detailed explanations for each phase  
- What to study, why, and how  
- Official documentation links  
- Weekly and daily instructions  
- Optional extended tasks  
- Validation checkpoints to track your progress  

---

## 📘 INTRODUCTION

This roadmap was created to help you:

1. Return to software development with confidence and consistency.  
2. Regain fluency in modern technologies (React, Next.js, TS, Node, Nest).  
3. Build two strong portfolio-ready projects that impress international recruiters.  
4. Strengthen your resume, GitHub, and technical presence.  
5. Prepare for technical interviews (Frontend and Full Stack).  
6. Stay organized with a sustainable routine: **3 hours per day**.

Strategy:

- **80% practical work** (real projects with clean code)  
- **20% study** (concise, official learning material)  
- No long courses  
- No wasted time  
- Only what truly prepares you for the global market
